# ARESTA — Design source of truth

*Locked 2026-07-17 in the first design session. Supersedes the working notes.*

## The one-line brief

**A hotel shaped by light, proportion, and silence.**

## Identity

**Name:** Aresta — Portuguese for the edge where two planes meet.
**Series:** Aubade (Hotel Two — architectural and composed).
**Place:** The cliff plateau above the Atlantic at Carrapateira, Costa
Vicentina, southwest Portugal.
**Proposition:** Architecture gives the guest a new way to see space.

The name is now inevitable rather than provisional: the hotel stands on the
arris where the plateau breaks into the sea cliff, and every deep window
reveal repeats that edge at the scale of a room. Lagar do Sol is inland
Alentejo morning; Aresta is the same country's ocean edge. Same doctrine,
opposite condition.

## The building

New construction. One long two-storey bar of pale in-situ concrete and
mineral plaster, set along the last contour of the plateau, its long wall
facing west to the Atlantic. Three planted courts are cut through the bar.
A limestone pool runs east–west on the south court, aligned to the horizon.
The lower hall, the stair, and the pool are cut from the same limestone.

**Governing geometry:** a 3.6-metre structural bay. Every room is two bays.
Window reveals are 90 cm deep — each one an aresta of plaster and shadow.
The facade is the module made visible: bay, reveal, bay, reveal.

**Program:** 38 rooms on two floors, restaurant, pool, three courts.

## Rooms by light — the room system

Rooms are classified by orientation, not marketing tier:

| Orientation | Light | Condition | Count |
|---|---|---|---:|
| EAST | First light | Sunrise over the inland heath; morning court | 12 |
| SOUTH | High light | Midday sun raking the pool-court reveals | 8 |
| WEST | Last light | The final two hours of light over the Atlantic | 18 |

Copy describes what the light does, in precise calm observation:
"West rooms receive the final two hours of light."

## Binding palette

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Plaster, lit | `#ece5d8` | Page ground — the wall in full sun |
| Sun-bleached flax | `#dfd4b4` | Linen fields, warm daylight surfaces |
| Mineral plaster | `#c2b9a8` | The wall in shade; frames and surfaces |
| Lichen stone | `#968e67` | Muted architectural accent; captions |
| Bronze oak | `#6b5c3b` | Joinery, grounded controls, depth |
| Deep eucalyptus | `#51685e` | Cool structural counterpoint; interaction |
| Schist | `#26221c` | Structural dark — text, frames, contrast |

Schist is the Carrapateira cliff rock: a warm near-black, chosen from the
place, verified for body contrast on every light ground. The lit/shaded
plaster pair is one material in two lights, not two colors. **No added clear
accent** — the Atlantic appears in photography; deep eucalyptus carries the
cool note in UI.

## Type

- **Archivo (variable, width axis)** — the structural voice. Compass labels,
  data, facts, wayfinding. Wide caps for EAST / SOUTH / WEST; normal width
  for body facts. Architecture speaks in this face.
- **Newsreader (optical size + italic)** — the editorial voice. Statements,
  room descriptions, the host's aside in italic. The human note.

Neither face is shared with Lagar do Sol. Avoid uppercase-everything,
extreme-contrast Didones, and tiny low-contrast body text.

## Grid

The page grid derives from the facade: a 12-column field read as bays.
Image planes land on bay multiples; hairline bay rules may appear as
structure where a section needs measure, never as decoration. Edges are
exact. Softness comes from light and material, not rounded UI.

## Signature interaction — orientation and light

The one bold spend. In **Rooms by light**, selecting EAST, SOUTH, or WEST
changes together: the room photograph, the sun's direction on a small room
plan (a light wedge at the correct azimuth), the hour band ("07:10 — first
light"), the facts (floor, bays, outlook), and the description. Light enters
the image frame from the sun's side. The interaction teaches the building;
it does not decorate booking.

Nothing else on the page competes with it. Motion elsewhere behaves like
sun and weather: slow, exact, settling into stillness, reduced-motion safe.

## Page arc

`The building → Rooms by light → Water → The table → Court → Place → Stay → Correspondence`

One long page. The hero reveals the governing proportion: the long west
wall cut by raking light, copy set in the structural negative space of sky
and wall — never in a floating card.

## Copy voice

Cooler than Lagar do Sol, never clinical. Authority through precise
observation: name the material, the hour, the direction, the count. No
"escape," no "curated," no luxury adjectives the building should prove.

## Never

Lagar's guest-day rail; inherited farmhouse furniture; repeated olive pots;
rustic tile motifs; generic beige hotel cards; glassmorphism; rounded
floating panels; masonry galleries; terracotta by reflex; ungrounded
architectural jargon; or a copied Aubade component library.
