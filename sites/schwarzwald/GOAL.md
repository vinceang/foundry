/goal

You are an expert at creating award-winning websites like those that win awwwards site of the day. Create a cinematic, ultra-premium ecommerce site for Schwarzwald, a legendary family atelier from the Black Forest. The site should feel like a rare private destination for wealthy collectors of authentic cuckoo clocks.

This is part of a series as in the README

This is not a normal shop. It should feel like a luxury brand, a heritage archive, and a high-end interactive game UI at once:
- immersive, interactive, and visually rich,
- elegant, exclusive, and meticulous,
- premium, elite, bespoke, and unmistakably exclusive,
- professional, collectible, and unforgettable,
- with enough wow-factor to impress gamers and luxury buyers alike.
- visually rich and cinematic,
- highly interactive and exploratory,
- polished enough to feel like a serious luxury brand,
- and dynamic enough to impress gamers who expect strong wow-factor.

Brand direction:
- evoke heritage, mastery, authenticity, precision, and prestige.
- Everything should feel curated, ceremonial, and bespoke.
- Emphasize craftsmen, craft, and meticulous workmanship.
- Avoid anything cheap, touristy, kitschy, or generic.
- Certificate of authenticity must be prominent and trusted.

Offerings:
- ready-made authentic Black Forest / Bavarian-style cuckoo clocks,
- bespoke made-to-order clocks,
- handcrafted details, provenance, and atelier-level customization.

Core pages or scenes (these are not hard titles. Please use your judgment and taste):
- Home.
- Collection.
- Bespoke.
- Craftsmanship.
- Certificate of Authenticity.  Why Authenticity Matters.
- About the Atelier.
- EN / Deutsch language versions.

Design direction:
- distinctive visual identity, refined palette, luxurious typography, and controlled dramatic motion.
- Strong atmosphere, layered composition, and deep product storytelling.
- Use advanced techniques: scroll storytelling, interactive clock exploration, layered parallax, cinematic transitions, custom navigation, premium microinteractions.
- Cuckoo clocks should feel like collectible objects of desire, not catalog items.
- Use real or convincingly generated assets where needed to support the premium feel.

Higgsfield usage:
- use Higgsfield selectively for cinematic hero visuals, motion loops, clock reveals, workshop storytelling, and premium product showcases.
- Use it only when motion or camera control materially improves the experience.
- Favor restraint; if still imagery is stronger, use stills.
- Treat Higgsfield as a premium visual amplifier, not a gimmick.

Image model usage:
- Take advantage of the latest image models for ornate clock renders, workshop scenes, material close-ups, and luxury product imagery.
- Use AI imagery to make the clocks feel dramatic, tactile, and desirable.

UX:
- The experience should feel exclusive, curated, and highly discriminating.
- Product pages should include craftsmanship notes, materials, movement details, provenance, certificate of authenticity, and bespoke options.
- The Certificate of Authenticity page must explain what the VdS certificate means, why mechanical construction matters, why Black Forest origin matters, and why collectors should care.
- The “Why Authenticity Matters” page should make the trust story feel elegant and essential.
- Navigation must feel sophisticated and clear.
- Mobile-first execution is required; mobile must feel as polished as desktop.
- Language switching must feel premium and seamless.

i18n:
- The site must support EN and Deutsch.
- Copy should feel native in both languages, not translated awkwardly.

Quality bar:
- Rare, confident, and unforgettable.
- Maximize wow-factor without becoming noisy or gimmicky.
- If anything feels generic, replace it with something more expressive and distinctive.
- Do not stop at the first draft: critique, fix at least five weaknesses, and revise 3 times.

Output:
- Brand concept and positioning.
- Visual direction.
- Interaction strategy.
- Core pages/sections.
- Why it feels premium, collectible, and award-worthy.
